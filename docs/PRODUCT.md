# Second: product brief

## The problem
Lots of people are great at their craft (massage, catering, training, hair, cleaning) but have no idea how to run the business side: pricing, paperwork, finding clients, marketing, tracking money. Many are middle-aged or older and not comfortable with tech. Existing tools assume you already know business and are comfortable wiring apps together.

## What Second is
An AI business partner that plans the work, does most of it, and asks the owner to approve. The owner never has to know what to ask. It feels like a warm, experienced mentor, not software.

## Who it's for (first)
- Solo and small local service businesses: massage and wellness, food and catering, personal trainers, hair and beauty, home services, photographers.
- Two groups: people who haven't started yet, and people who have started but don't know much about business.
- Design test user: "Maria," a massage therapist on the Jersey Shore.

## Two modes

### Launch mode (haven't started yet)
Goal: get them to opening day with confidence.
- **Mode picker** at signup: "I'm just getting started" vs. "My business is up and running."
- **Launch plan (home):** progress toward opening day (7 stages: Idea, Plan, Make it official, Money setup, Brand, First clients, Launch), this week's 3 steps, opening day countdown, money plan summary, "your business so far," brand kit status, common beginner questions.
- **Roadmap steps:** each stage breaks into small tasks with time estimates, plain-English explainers (sole proprietor vs. LLC, EIN, licenses, insurance), "Second's take," and links to official sites. Second prepares; the user files. Nothing is submitted without the user.
- **Money plan:** monthly costs, suggested price vs. a local range, sessions per month to cover costs and to hit their pay goal, savings goal for startup costs.
- **Brand kit:** name ideas, logo, colors, one-page website, Google Business Profile, booking link.
- **Launch day handoff:** celebration screen, shows what moves over to Running mode and what's new, button into the Running dashboard.

### Running mode (already open)
Goal: do the busywork and grow.
- **Dashboard (home base):** this week's game plan (3 drafted moves with approve buttons, "Approve all"), money this month with a 6-month chart, stat tiles (bookings, new clients, outreach replies, time saved), new replies, today's schedule, ideas from Second.
- **Outreach:** finds local businesses to partner with (gyms, studios, offices), writes personalized emails, tracks them on a board: waiting, replied, booked. Next batch ready to review.
- **Money:** earned, spent, kept; monthly chart; where money went; recent activity; tips.
- **Content:** this week's Instagram posts with photo ideas, captions, copy buttons.
- **Clients** (later): client memory, rebooking, review requests.

### Shared by both modes
- **Chat with Second:** ask anything in plain words; Second can answer with tappable options and drafts it can send with approval. Side panel shows what Second is working on and the business profile.
- **Learn:** short lessons shown when relevant.
- Business profile (type, area, services, prices, goals).

## Principles
- Second drafts, the user approves. Never send, post, or file anything without approval.
- One clear next step at all times. Overwhelm is the enemy.
- Plain English everywhere. Explain any business word on tap.
- Celebrate progress.

## Later ideas (not in early phases)
Voice mode, text-message-only mode, snap a photo of receipts or handwritten notes, family helper access, scam shield, tax set-aside reminders, "what matters today" button, review requests, local networking and mentoring finder.
