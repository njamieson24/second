# Second

Second is an AI business partner for small business owners, especially people who are great at their craft but new to the business side (think a 55-year-old massage therapist). Tagline: "Your business partner, in your pocket."

Read @docs/PRODUCT.md for what we're building and @docs/DESIGN.md for how it must look. The approved mockups are in `design/screens/` (PNG) and `design/html/` (HTML source). Match them closely.

## Two modes (core concept)
- **Launch mode**: for people who haven't opened yet. Roadmap to opening day, money plan, brand kit.
- **Running mode**: for people already in business. Weekly game plan, outreach, money tracking, content.
- `/` is the intro page (what Second is). "Get started" goes to `/signup`; "Try the demo" needs no account. Then `/start` (pick a mode) and `/start/setup` (a few questions). Finishing Launch mode "graduates" them to Running mode and carries their data over.

## Stack
- Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
- Font: Outfit (via `next/font/google`). Icons: `lucide-react`.
- Accounts and data: Supabase (email + password login, Postgres). Tables and row level security are in `supabase/schema.sql` (plus `calendar.sql`, `beta.sql`, `chat.sql`, `memory.sql`, `week.sql`, `content.sql`, `content-v2.sql`, `outreach.sql`, `chat-outreach.sql`, `outreach-cleanup.sql`, `catalog.sql`, `catalog-b.sql`).
- "Try the demo" keeps the old behavior: data stays in this browser (localStorage), no account.
- IMPORTANT: `src/lib/store.ts` is the ONLY file that saves or loads data, and the only one that signs people in or out. It picks the backend: `store-supabase.ts` when signed in, `store-local.ts` in the demo. Pages read data through `src/lib/data.tsx` (`useLoadedData`, `useRoadmap`, `RequireAccount`, `RequireBusiness`) and write by calling store functions. Pages never touch localStorage or Supabase directly. (Exceptions: the before-paint script in `src/lib/boot.ts`, and the light/dark choice in `src/lib/theme.tsx`, which is a device setting, not business data.)
- Supabase keys live in `.env.local` (git-ignored; see `.env.local.example`). Only the PUBLISHABLE key goes in the app. NEVER use the secret / service_role key in browser code, and never commit keys or test passwords.
- REMINDER: "Confirm email" is turned OFF in Supabase (Authentication > Sign In / Providers > Email) to make testing easy. Turn it back ON before real customers use the app. The sign-up page already handles it ("Check your email").
- Before real customers: also set the real Site URL and Redirect URLs in Authentication > URL Configuration, and set up a custom email sender (Supabase's built-in one only sends a few emails per hour).
- Catalog (what the business sells): `supabase/catalog.sql` (`catalog_items` + `catalog_variants`, owner-only), helpers in `src/lib/catalog.ts`, server reads in `src/lib/catalog-server.ts`. The ONE source of truth for prices: nothing writes `businesses.services` anymore (left in place, unused). `business.services` is still there to read, but store.ts and the AI routes (`withCatalog`) fill it from the catalog (one entry per active variant). Onboarding (`ensureCatalog`), the Settings editor (`CatalogEditor`), the money plan's main price and the chat cards (set_price / add_service / remove_service) all write the catalog. Prices $0 to $10,000, entries and daily totals up to $100,000, max 2 decimals (plain `numeric` columns so more decimals are rejected, not rounded). Deleting hides (`active = false`); variants are never deleted. Money entries keep their own amount and label (history never changes).
- Money entries from the catalog: item, variant, quantity, `unit_price`, discount and tip (tips kept separate). The database computes `amount = unit_price x quantity - discount + tip` in the `transactions_from_parts` trigger and checks the item, variant and appointment are the owner's own; never trust a total from the browser. "Got paid" on a past calendar appointment (`EventDialog`) logs income with `event_id` + `event_date` (unique, so it can't be logged twice) and uses the appointment's price, else the catalog's. Quick-add: `src/components/money/QuickIncome.tsx` (Repeat last, most used in 90 days, all items, big targets). Dates are the device's local date.
- Logging money from chat: the `suggest_income` tool (`src/lib/chat-tools.ts`). Second gets the catalog as IDs (`catalogRefs`: V1 = "Aromatherapy, 60 min, $120") and must return one; the server rejects any other ID (including real database IDs it wasn't given), future dates, more than 60 days back, and totals outside $0 to $100,000. If the item has several options and the owner's message doesn't say which, the server makes no card and Second asks ("Which Deep tissue was it: 60 min ($110) or 90 min ($150)?"). The "Log income" card (`log_income`, allowed by `catalog-b.sql`) saves nothing until Approve; Approve logs it through `addTransaction` (the database computes the total) only if that option is still on sale.
- "Find prices Second already knows" (Settings, `PriceDraft`): `src/app/api/catalog/route.ts` + `src/lib/catalog-extract.ts`. Only when tapped; one Haiku call (1,500 tokens) reading business notes and approved price cards only (never chat history); 5 a day, logged in `catalog_runs`. Haiku returns lines by catalog ID (I1 item, V1 option) with a cited source (N1 note, A1 card). The server drops unknown IDs or sources, prices outside $0 to $10,000, prices not written in the cited source, and duplicates; a price for an item with several options and no clear option becomes a question. The owner reviews (edit prices, uncheck lines) and approves once; `applyCatalogDraft` saves through `saveCatalogItem`. The demo shows a made-up draft (no AI).
- Kinds of business: `src/lib/business-kinds.ts` maps each business type to a family (appointment, food, retail, general) with its words (client/customer, session/order/sale, service/menu item/product, "Services and prices"/"Menu and prices"), catalog shape and default logging. Components read words from here; never hard-code massage or appointment wording. Part A uses the words only; prompts, dashboard, logging style and onboarding types follow in later parts.
- Roadmap stages and tasks: `src/lib/roadmap.ts`. Business types, suggested services, prices and costs: `src/lib/business-types.ts`. Money math: `src/lib/money.ts`.
- `src/lib/mock-data.ts` now only holds sample content for features that need AI or outside services (game plan, outreach, content posts, chat, schedule, tips).
- `/settings` shows the account (Log out) or the demo (Create an account / Leave the demo), and "Start over" (deletes business data, keeps the login).
- `/beta` is the public beta sign-up page (no login). Rows go to `beta_signups` (`supabase/beta.sql`): anonymous visitors can only insert, nobody can read it from the browser. The page saves through `joinBeta` in store.ts, which posts to `src/app/api/beta/route.ts`. Never `.select()` after that insert or upsert; a duplicate email (error 23505) counts as success. Form rules: `src/lib/beta.ts`. Preview image: `src/app/beta/opengraph-image.tsx` (fonts in `assets/`); it needs `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` (the live address) in `.env.local` and Vercel.
- Test accounts made by automated tests use `second-test-<number>@example.com`. Delete them with `supabase/delete-test-accounts.sql`.

## AI (Claude API)
- Chat with Second: Server route: `src/app/api/chat/route.ts` (official `@anthropic-ai/sdk`). Personality and business context: `src/lib/chat-context.ts`. Limits and the stream format: `src/lib/chat.ts`. The page calls `getChatHistory` / `sendChatMessage` in store.ts.
- IMPORTANT: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is read ONLY in server route code. Never import it into a "use client" file, never give it a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` name, never log it, never commit it.
- Model: `CLAUDE_MODEL` (server-only), default `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`. Thinking models (like `claude-sonnet-5-5`) automatically get low effort, a bigger reply cap, and Anthropic's refusal fallback.
- Credit limits: 600 tokens per reply (1500 for thinking models), last 20 messages as history, 2,000 characters per message, 50 messages per person per 24 hours (`CHAT_DAILY_LIMIT` overrides). Token use is saved on each reply in `chat_messages`; the cost query is at the bottom of `supabase/chat.sql`.
- "Try the demo" never calls the AI. It shows a sample conversation and a sign-up button.
- Chat history sent to Claude: only the last 20 messages from the last 24 hours (`CHAT_HISTORY_HOURS` overrides). Older facts live on as business notes, so deleting a note really makes Second forget it.
- Suggested updates: Second proposes changes with tools (`src/lib/chat-tools.ts`), the server checks them and saves cards in `chat_proposals`. The model never writes. Only the owner's Approve writes, through store.ts (`approveProposal` uses `saveBusiness`, `savePayGoal`, `addEvent`, `updateEvent`, `deleteEvent`). Second must never say something changed before it's approved.
- Business notes ("What Second knows" in Settings): `src/app/api/memory/route.ts` + `src/lib/notes-pass.ts`. Vercel has no background jobs, so the chat page triggers a pass when it opens and every 6 messages; the server skips it if nothing is new. Always Haiku, 300-token cap, max 3 changes, 8 passes per 24 hours (`NOTES_DAILY_LIMIT`). Tokens logged in `memory_runs`. Never save client details, health details or account numbers (instructions plus a server-side filter). Never log note text or messages.

- "This Week" (weekly game plan, `src/components/ThisWeek.tsx` at the top of Home in both modes): `src/app/api/week/route.ts`. Made the first time Home opens each week (Monday start, owner's time zone); read from the database after that, so inactive owners cost nothing. Plain code works out the facts (`src/lib/week-facts.ts`: money vs. pay goal and pace, days with nothing booked, clients not back in 5+ weeks from calendar appointments and income "Who was it?" names, notes, last week's Done/Skip); one Haiku call picks exactly 3 actions with a tool; the server checks them (`src/lib/week-plan.ts`: types, lengths, a real number in every "why", mode rules, at most 1 collab idea and only when open, no invented business names or distances), retries once, then falls back to a plan made by plain code. A try is claimed in `weekly_plans` (unique on user + week + attempt) BEFORE the AI call, so two tabs can't make or pay for two plans. 3 AI tries a week (first + 2 redos); after that, and after Start over with no tries left, the plain-code plan is used (never an empty card). "Do it with Second" drafts: one Haiku call each (150 to 300 tokens), checked (client messages use the first name only; no contact details), saved on the action, editable, with Copy. At most 15 drafts a day (`DRAFTS_DAILY_LIMIT`). Clients are only ever sent to Claude by first name.
- Client texting rules (server-side, `applyTextingRules` in `src/lib/week-facts.ts`, so held-back clients never reach Claude): after a check-in text is marked Done, that client rests 3 weeks; after 2 Done texts with no new visit or payment from them, stop suggesting them until they book or pay again. Skipped texts follow the skip rule (not suggested again the next week).
- Content page (`/content`, both modes; `/app/content` forwards there): `src/app/api/content/route.ts`, rules in `src/lib/content-plan.ts`. Each idea is a short creative brief: what to post, format (photo, carousel or reel), 2 or 3 shots the owner takes on their own phone, a hook (first caption line, or on-screen text for a Reel's first 3 seconds), and the best day. 5 briefs are made the first time Content opens each week in one Haiku call (1,200-token cap), same pattern as This Week: facts from `src/lib/server-facts.ts` minus client names, a try claimed in `content_weeks` before the AI call, 2 "New ideas" a week, plain-code fallback briefs (`briefFor`). Every brief is checked on the server: no health claims, only saved prices, no invented offers or percentages, no client names, no invented business names, phone only (no tripod, ring light, gimbal, etc.) and one person (nobody else filming, no clients in the shots). This Week's post action is pinned as the first idea and captions sync both ways. "Write caption" = one Haiku call (300 tokens) for the caption and hashtags that follow the hook, with the same checks plus 5 to 8 hashtags with a local one; counts toward `DRAFTS_DAILY_LIMIT` (logged in `draft_runs` as 'caption'). Owners can edit the hook (saved through the server after the same checks) and the caption; shots and format are read-only. Copy = hook, blank line, caption with hashtags. Tone (warm, fun, professional) is `businesses.content_tone`.
- No image templates or brand colors: removed on purpose (they live in git history, commit 791bde5). The old columns `content_posts.template`, `template_size`, `template_text` and `businesses.brand_primary`, `brand_accent` are left in the database, unused.
- `PLAN_WEEK_OFFSET` is a TEST-ONLY server setting that pretends it's N weeks later. Never set it in Vercel.
- Outreach (`/outreach`, both modes; `/app/outreach` forwards there): the page only lists businesses the owner saved from Chat with Second ("Save to Outreach"), plus a "Find more with Second" button. The page's own map search and "Add a business yourself" are HIDDEN on purpose (it felt cluttered), not deleted: `SearchPanel` and `AddLeadForm` are still in `src/components/outreach/` and the `/api/outreach` search and add_manual actions still work. `supabase/outreach-cleanup.sql` took old untouched search results out of the list (saved=false). Details of the search, kept for chat and for bringing it back: `src/app/api/outreach/route.ts`, tables in `supabase/outreach.sql`. Second NEVER sends anything: no SMTP, no email APIs. The owner copies the draft (or "Open in my email app", a mailto link) and sends it from their own email, then taps "I sent it".
  - Find businesses: Nominatim turns the town into an OpenStreetMap area (cached per person in `outreach_towns`; at most 1 lookup a second for the whole app via `claim_nominatim_slot()`), then Nominatim lists businesses of that kind within 5, 10, 15 or 20 miles of the town's middle (default 10; `nominatimPlaces` in `src/lib/outreach-osm.ts`). Nominatim doesn't sort by distance, so it searches growing boxes (2, 5, 10, 15 miles, then the full distance) with `[amenity=cafe]`-style queries, 40 per page, at most 12 requests a search, each taking a turn from `claim_nominatim_slot()`; results are sorted by distance and the closest 150 are kept. Each lead stores lat/lon; searches are reused only for the same town, kind, distance and Hide chains. Map tags per category are in `CATEGORIES` (`src/lib/outreach.ts`) and `NOMINATIM_TAGS`: never `leisure=sports_centre` (pools, racetracks, ball fields) or `leisure=spa` (pools, hot tubs); bump `CATEGORY_CHANGED` when a category's tags change so old searches aren't reused. A complete fresh search removes saved places the map no longer lists there, but only untouched ones (still New, no email, note or draft). The same business mapped twice (same name within 0.2 miles) is shown once. Overpass is only the backup (if Nominatim is down or finds nothing): it was returning 504 for every query for hours, so it must never be the only source. "Hide chains" (on by default) skips places tagged with a brand. Never lists the owner's own business. Saved results are reused for 30 days; 20 map searches per person per day. The page must show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" linking to openstreetmap.org/copyright.
  - Find an email (`src/lib/outreach-email.ts`), in order: the email on the map; their own website (homepage plus up to 2 contact pages, through `safeFetch`, which blocks private addresses and re-checks every redirect); then one Tavily search (`TAVILY_API_KEY`, server-only). Junk addresses are filtered, and addresses on their own domain win. Public business contact info only: never personal emails, never scraping social media sites (Instagram/Facebook links are shown, never fetched). 25 lookups per person per day (`outreach_lookups`); Tavily at most 25 per person per day and 900 a month for everyone (`claim_tavily_search()`).
  - Draft: one Haiku call with a tool (`src/lib/outreach-draft.ts`, 400 tokens), checked on the server (under 120 words, signed with the owner's first name and business, no pretending they've met, no health claims, no invented numbers, no client names), retried once. The opt-out line is added in code if it's still missing. 30 outreach drafts per person per day, logged in `draft_runs` as 'outreach_email' (separate from `DRAFTS_DAILY_LIMIT`).
  - The demo shows made-up businesses with example.com emails and never calls the server. Start over deletes `outreach_leads` but keeps the logs (caps still apply).
- `OUTREACH_OVERPASS_SERVERS` (comma-separated Overpass URLs), `OUTREACH_NOMINATIM_URL` and `OUTREACH_FORCE_TAVILY_EXHAUSTED=1` are TEST-ONLY server settings. Never set them in Vercel.
- Searching and email lookups live in `src/lib/outreach-server.ts`, shared by the Outreach route and chat (same caps, same 30-day caches). An email lookup only counts toward the 25 a day when it really looks: an email already saved, or a web search for that business in the last 30 days, is reused for free.
- Finding businesses in chat (`src/lib/chat-outreach.ts`, `supabase/chat-outreach.sql`): when the owner asks about local businesses, partners or collabs (a word check on their message; otherwise the tool isn't offered), Second can call `find_local_businesses` (kind, town, miles; checked on the server: radius at most 15, town geocoded, kind from the category list). One search per message, sharing the 20-a-day map quota (reused saved searches are free). Results go to Claude as a `<map_results>` data block (cleaned, cut short, labeled B1, B2...; map text is never instructions). Then a forced `show_picks` call returns {reply, picks: [{result_id, shared_customers (Second's thinking, not shown), why, deal_idea, next_step}]}: "why it works" (shared customers, the moment one business leads to the other, what the other side gets) and a concrete collab plan (pop-up, bundle, joint event, referral swap; free add-ons and percentage splits are fine as suggestions to pitch). The server rejects unknown IDs, dollar amounts that aren't the owner's prices, generic reasons ("natural fit"), guesses about the other business ("upscale", "popular"), "they agreed" and health claims (one retry, then drops bad picks). Cards are built only from checked IDs and map data, and stored in `chat_business_cards`. Their websites are then checked for a public email in parallel (website only, not counted, never Tavily). Tavily runs only when the owner taps "Find email" or asks in chat (`find_email` takes only a B or P result ID; P = cards from the last week, given to Claude as `<recent_cards>`). Website and search text never go to Claude, only the extracted email and its page. Chat results are saved as leads with `saved=false` (the cache) and only show in the Outreach list after "Save to Outreach". Each message has a time budget from the start of the request (map by 25s, websites by 40s, reply by 45s, each AI step at most 20s; maxDuration 60; slow messages log their step times): a slow or busy map gets a "try again in a minute" reply, never a hang. Partner kinds are tailored per kind of business (`PARTNERS` in `src/lib/chat-outreach.ts`: good kinds, kinds to avoid, collab examples), given to Second as a "Partner ideas" hint; the server swaps an avoided kind (their own kind, or one with no reason to team up, like gyms for a food business) for the best partner kind. Business-specific examples live only there, never in the general rules. Kinds include Mini golf & fun, Breweries & wineries, Hotels & inns, and Event venues & theaters. Any collab/partner/deal request searches first (not "how do I..." questions). Thinking models (CLAUDE_MODEL=sonnet...) get the results but no cards (forced tool use isn't allowed with thinking).
- Known limit: Haiku occasionally asks "want me to update it?" in words without making a card, so the owner has no way to say yes. The rules tell it "the card IS the question", which fixed it in testing, but it can still happen. A stronger model (`CLAUDE_MODEL=claude-sonnet-5-5`) should be more reliable.
- Prompt caching: not used yet. The fixed part of every chat request (persona + rules 689 tokens, 7 tools 1,458 tokens = 2,147, measured with count_tokens) is below Haiku 4.5's 4,096-token minimum, so a cache marker would do nothing. Order is already cache-friendly (tools, then the fixed system block, then the per-request business context). If the prompt grows past 4,096 tokens, or when switching to a model with a lower minimum (Sonnet 5.5 is 512), add `cache_control` to the first system block and check `cache_read_input_tokens` in `chat_messages`.

- "Take a tour" (`src/components/Tour.tsx`, steps in `src/lib/tour.ts`): walks through the real pages for the current mode. Each step opens its page and points at an element marked `data-tour="..."` (or `target: "page"` = the page title; every PageHeader has `data-tour-header`). The page is dimmed and can't be clicked during the tour; Esc ends it, arrows go back and forth. Started from "Take a tour" in the sidebar/phone menu, and offered once on Home by `TourOffer` (remembered on this device through store.ts `wasTourOffered`/`markTourOffered`). When a page or feature changes, update its step text in tour.ts.

## Before public launch
- Switch chat message saving to a server-only Supabase secret key (never `NEXT_PUBLIC_`, never in browser code), and remove the "Add own rows" insert policy on `chat_messages`, so users can't insert fake rows into their own history. Today the server saves AS the user with the publishable key. The same applies to `chat_proposals`, `business_notes` and `memory_runs` (memory.sql), `weekly_plans`, `plan_actions` and `draft_runs` (week.sql), `content_weeks` and `content_posts` (content.sql), `outreach_leads`, `outreach_towns`, `outreach_searches` and `outreach_lookups` (outreach.sql), and `chat_business_cards` (chat-outreach.sql): move their server-side inserts to the secret key too and drop the "Add own rows" insert policies. Same for `catalog_runs` (catalog-b.sql).
- Outreach website fetching (`safeFetch` in `src/lib/outreach-email.ts`) checks the address a name points to and then fetches it, so a site could switch its DNS in between (DNS rebinding) and reach a private address. Accepted for now because Vercel functions have no private network to reach. Before launch, pin the checked IP for the connection (custom DNS lookup on the request) or fetch through an outbound proxy that blocks private ranges.

## Future features
- Business hours should become a real field (days of the week and open/close times, not free text) when we build calendar booking. Booking and Seconds setting up events with each other both need to know when someone is open. Until then, hours mentioned in chat are saved as business notes.

- Collab ideas in "This Week" are the solo version of Seconds talking to each other: Second suggests a TYPE of business ("a gym near you") and drafts a pitch the owner sends themselves. Later this becomes Second-to-Second (each owner's Second talks to the other business's Second), and suggesting real nearby businesses needs a places API (like Google Places). Until then, never invent business names, addresses or distances.

## Rules
- IMPORTANT: Build only what the current phase in @docs/BUILD_PLAN.md asks for. Don't add features, libraries, or pages that weren't requested.
- All colors, radii, and fonts come from the tokens in docs/DESIGN.md (set up once in the Tailwind theme). Never hardcode one-off hex values in components.
- Reuse components (Sidebar, Card, Chip, Button, StatTile) instead of copying markup between pages.
- Copy is plain English for non-technical, older users. No jargon, no "leverage/seamless/unlock." Short sentences.
- Accessibility: real `<button>` and `<a>` elements, visible focus states, text contrast at least 4.5:1, touch targets at least 44px.
- Every page must also work at phone width (sidebar collapses).
- Every page must work in light and dark mode (see "Light and dark mode" in docs/DESIGN.md). Sidebar, hero cards, and other panels use `bg-panel text-on-panel` (pale in light mode, ink in dark mode; this intentionally differs from the dark sidebar in the mockups), never `bg-ink`. Use `text-on-sky` / `text-on-green` on filled buttons, never `text-white`.
- Scripts that must run before paint (theme, mode) live in `src/lib/boot.ts`, not in a "use client" file. Exports from client files can't be inlined as text.
- When you finish UI work, take a screenshot of the page and compare it to the matching file in `design/screens/`. List the differences and fix them before reporting done. The mockups are light mode only, so also screenshot dark mode and check it reads well.
